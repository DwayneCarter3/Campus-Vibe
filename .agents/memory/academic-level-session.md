Use the 2025/26 academic session (start year 2025) when interpreting matric entry years until the active CampusX session is explicitly changed. A 2023 matric entry is 300L in this session, not 400L.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected the 2025/26 session over calendar year 2026 for automatic calculation, even though the current calendar date is in 2026.

**How to apply:** Treat the session start year as a product decision when updating academic-level calculations; do not silently advance it on January 1. Preserve a saved manual level as the user's override.
