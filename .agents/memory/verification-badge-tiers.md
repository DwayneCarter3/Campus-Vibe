---
name: Verification badge tiers
description: Assignment rule for student, business, and optional gold verification styling.
---

The gold vendor badge is a visual option, not an automatically assigned account tier. Marketplace vendor names use the business verification label with the standard blue badge unless a genuine paid business-vendor entitlement is introduced.

**Why:** Student verification and marketplace listing promotion do not establish a top-tier paid business-vendor entitlement. Inferring gold from either would misrepresent the account.

**How to apply:** When adding business-tier features, define and check a dedicated entitlement before assigning gold; otherwise keep verified vendor badges blue.
