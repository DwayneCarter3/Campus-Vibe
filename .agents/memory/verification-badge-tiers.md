description: Latest public tick hierarchy and approval policy.
---

The latest user direction supersedes free Student Verified pills and public premium role tags. Show only one tick beside a name for an active Green, Gold/Yellow, or Premium Blue tier. Gold is a distinct paid 30-day plan, not a visual alias of Blue. Internal CEO/Admin/Moderator access never implies a public tick; an explicit time-limited admin grant can create one. The verified designated founder alone gets a lifetime choice among the three colors without paying or renewing.

**Why:** Public staff labels and automatically inferred checkmarks caused confusion about which accounts had actually purchased or been approved for verification.

**How to apply:** Derive visible ticks from an approved public entitlement and expiry, not role or baseline status. Keep pending checkout/review states badge-free; use the payment-validated approval route for purchased tiers and a separate explicit admin grant for manual awards. Treat the founder exception as a separately granted lifetime entitlement backed by fresh verified identity, not as a role badge.
