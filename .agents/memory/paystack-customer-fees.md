---
name: Paystack customer fees
description: Avoid double-charging when passing transaction fees to CampusX buyers.
---

For Nigerian Paystack local transactions, either calculate and include the processing fee in the checkout price or use Paystack's dashboard setting that automatically passes fees to customers. Do not use both. International transaction fees can differ from the local estimate.

**Why:** Paystack's own guidance describes these as alternative fee-passing methods; turning on dashboard pass-through in addition to a local price gross-up charges the customer twice.

**How to apply:** Keep dashboard automatic pass-through off while the app shows a fee-inclusive checkout total. Verify charged amount/currency against the initialized amount, and clearly disclose local fee assumptions to the merchant.
