---
name: Responsive management parity
description: CampusX phone and tablet management must match desktop capability.
---

CampusX must provide the same working management functions on phones and tablets as on desktop, including Admin tools, CGPA plans, and marketplace listing management. Do not hide functionality merely to fit smaller screens.

**Why:** The user explicitly required full desktop/mobile functional parity, accessible navigation, and no broken layouts or horizontal page overflow.

**How to apply:** Preserve role-based access while adapting navigation, forms, dialogs, and action groups for small screens. Include narrow phones and tablets when verifying new management tools.
