---
name: Dispatch actor and audience
description: Keep automated news attribution separate from student identity and visibility.
---

Automated institutional news uses one reserved system identity, not a fabricated Clerk student. A fixed actor campus cannot determine who should see its posts: the institution-wide audience must be recorded on each news post and checked on both lists and detail reads. Reserve a negative registration rank so automation never consumes a first-100 student benefit.

**Why:** A shared bot that lives at one campus would hide other schools' news or leak posts through inconsistent profile/detail queries. Counting it among student registrations would also distort early-bird eligibility.

**How to apply:** Keep bot identity server-owned and non-authenticatable; separate news targeting from the actor's profile and preserve ordinary student school-plus-campus scoping.
