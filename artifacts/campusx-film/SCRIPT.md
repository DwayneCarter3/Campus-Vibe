# CampusX — One Campus, Every Connection

## Director's treatment

**Format:** 16:9 landscape, 26 seconds. **Audience:** students exploring CampusX. **Promise:** a single place to connect to campus life. **Identity:** existing CampusX network-X mark, #FF2E93 to #FF6B00 gradient, deep plum and pale cream; Space Grotesk display and Inter supporting text. **Hero:** the brand's network X becomes the structure that connects different student experiences. **Motion:** traced connections, object-scale match cuts, and tightly timed typography. **Arc:** fast network hook → concrete campus moments → gathered brand resolution. **Payoff:** the feature fragments converge into the real CampusX logo on a quiet, high-contrast field. No pricing, availability, or usage claims. One instrumental electronic/afro-pop inspired bed; no narration or SFX.

### Shot 01 / 0.00–3.20 / 3.20 s — An X made of people
- **Purpose:** Establish CampusX as a network, not a generic lettermark.
- **Exact content:** “YOUR CAMPUS.” appears at 0.40, “CONNECTED.” at 1.35; small “CAMPUSX / THE STUDENT NETWORK” at 2.20. Hold the large copy until 3.05. No narration.
- **Composition + idea:** Deep plum field with oversized real X icon silhouette center-right and a narrow type zone left. Four white nodes orbit the ends of the X strokes. The center connection is the visual focal point; a faint grid recedes behind, never competing with the copy.
- **Choreography:** 0.00–0.40: near-black X strokes snap outward from center, first two nodes light. 0.40–1.10: the first title unclips as remaining nodes draw connected strokes. 1.10–1.75: the center node ignites in the brand gradient and “CONNECTED.” fills the lower-left. 1.75–2.20: subtle scale pullback exposes whole network. 2.20–2.65: tiny descriptor draws on a horizontal rule. 2.65–3.20: center node scales and moves toward camera while title shears/fades, exposing the next shot; incoming feed card starts before the X disappears.
- **Transition contract:** The X center node becomes the circular avatar dot at the top of the feed card; a gradient radial aperture and a scale push overlap.
- **Assets + technique:** Reuse logo component mark geometry, SVG nodes/lines, masked type. No generated imagery.
- **Sound intent:** A brief filtered intro, then the pulse enters; beat alignment unverified.

### Shot 02 / 3.20–8.20 / 5.00 s — Campus Gist
- **Purpose:** Show student conversation in motion.
- **Exact content:** “EVERY STORY” at 3.55, “HAS A CAMPUS.” at 4.15, “CAMPUS GIST” at 4.95. Feed-card copy: “What’s happening on campus?”, “Amebo Hot”, “That exam timetable update…”, “Anyone at the library today?” visible by 5.55. “REAL CAMPUS CONVERSATIONS” at 6.65. Hold until 7.95.
- **Composition + idea:** Cream background. Oversized editorial title on the left; layered, noninteractive feed cards occupy right ~60%, angled just enough for depth. Rather than a phone screenshot, the feed itself grows from the node. Pink and orange accents label two conversations.
- **Choreography:** 3.20–3.80: initial card scales out of the incoming node; first line reveals. 3.80–4.60: second card unfolds from behind while second title lands. 4.60–5.55: category chip and two content rows resolve sequentially. 5.55–6.50: the camera pushes closer; a reaction circle pulses once from a specific card. 6.50–7.20: lower caption traces in; 7.20–8.20: reaction circle expands with a soft radial wash and the cards recede, carrying the next shot.
- **Transition contract:** Reaction circle becomes the map locator pulse. Cream/pink field gives way to plum map via circle wipe and camera zoom.
- **Assets + technique:** CSS cards and SVG icons; real app feature names. UI is static artwork, not clickable.
- **Sound intent:** Bouncy rhythmic bed with a small accent at the circle pulse; timing unverified.

### Shot 03 / 8.20–12.80 / 4.60 s — Shuttle movement
- **Purpose:** Make practical campus movement feel immediate.
- **Exact content:** “MOVE WITH” at 8.55, “YOUR CAMPUS.” at 9.25, “SHUTTLE UPDATES” at 10.05, “Know what’s moving.” at 11.20. Hold until 12.55.
- **Composition + idea:** Deep plum cartographic field. A bold curved route occupies most of the right two-thirds, with fixed luminous stops and one capsule marker traveling along it. Big title is left; supporting copy sits bottom-left. Schematic route, not a claim about live vehicle tracking.
- **Choreography:** 8.20–8.65: previous circle resolves into first stop; route draws from it. 8.65–9.55: capsule travels the path and second stop blinks as title reveals in two beats. 9.55–10.35: “SHUTTLE UPDATES” locks to route; line continues around a bend. 10.35–11.25: marker advances to final stop; pulse rings travel out. 11.25–12.80: route straightens into a bright horizontal divider while map zooms off and the cream market field expands behind.
- **Transition contract:** The route's last horizontal segment remains at the same screen height as the divider of the first marketplace card; camera pullback plus colored-panel unfold.
- **Assets + technique:** SVG path with stroke-dash reveal, deterministic waypoint markers and CSS/text layout.
- **Sound intent:** Music build continues; no literal engine or horn.

### Shot 04 / 12.80–17.40 / 4.60 s — Student marketplace
- **Purpose:** Show goods moving between students without inventing sales figures.
- **Exact content:** “FIND IT.” at 13.05, “PASS IT ON.” at 13.70, “STUDENT MARKETPLACE” at 14.25; product-card labels “Textbooks”, “Room essentials”, “Electronics” from 14.7–15.3; “Made for campus life.” at 16.10. Hold until 17.10.
- **Composition + idea:** Warm cream field with an enormous coral-red outlined label extending just outside the top frame. Three graphic product panels form a diagonal from left-bottom to right-top. A hero textbook card expands to roughly 70% of short-edge height as the other two tuck behind it.
- **Choreography:** 12.80–13.30: previous route divider folds into the first product panel; title punches in. 13.30–14.15: the second phrase replaces its outline with solid ink; two new tiles rotate slightly into position. 14.15–15.20: labels appear sequentially as panels tilt and settle. 15.20–16.10: hero textbook tile enlarges while side tiles recoil. 16.10–17.40: caption arrives, then textbook tile turns into a flat grid cell as the camera pulls through it.
- **Transition contract:** Hero tile's rectangular silhouette and grid lines remain into the academic-plan shot; its scale collapses as four study blocks unfold.
- **Assets + technique:** Simple CSS/SVG pictograms and shapes, not generated product photography; this is software-interface art, not a physical-product ad.
- **Sound intent:** A short lift midway through the bed; alignment unverified.

### Shot 05 / 17.40–21.60 / 4.20 s — Study and WAZOBIA
- **Purpose:** Show that CampusX also helps students organize and ask questions.
- **Exact content:** “PLAN THE NEXT MOVE.” at 17.70, “ACADEMIC PLANNING” at 18.45, tiles “Courses”, “Goals”, “Progress” by 19.20; “ASK WAZOBIA” at 19.60 and speech artwork “How can I help?” at 20.30. Hold until 21.30.
- **Composition + idea:** A graphic grid in pale pink rises from the collapsed marketplace tile. The hero grid occupies the lower half; then a dark circular chat bubble overlays its top-right. A short headline stays at upper-left, avoiding overlap with the bubble.
- **Choreography:** 17.40–18.20: tile divides into grid cells; headline reveals. 18.20–19.15: cell labels appear one by one as a progress ring traces in. 19.15–20.10: the ring becomes a chat bubble and the “ASK WAZOBIA” callout draws from it. 20.10–20.85: response artwork unfolds inside bubble; a pink spark escapes. 20.85–21.60: camera follows the spark as the grid contracts toward the center; a deep plum aperture opens under it.
- **Transition contract:** The spark becomes the central white node of the branded network X; grid lines align briefly with X strokes before disappearing.
- **Assets + technique:** CSS grid, SVG ring/speech illustration; WAZOBIA label reflects the existing feature, no generated answers.
- **Sound intent:** Arrangement briefly thins, then returns for the finale; alignment unverified.

### Shot 06 / 21.60–26.00 / 4.40 s — One campus, every connection
- **Purpose:** Resolve the variety into one clear brand promise.
- **Exact content:** “ONE CAMPUS.” at 22.05, “EVERY CONNECTION.” at 22.65, authentic gradient CampusX SVG logo at 23.50, “Campus life, connected.” at 24.10. Last tagline holds through 25.75. No narration.
- **Composition + idea:** The spark reveals a near-black background. Four abstract fragments from feed, route, market and study converge, then vanish into the real vector logo, centered wide; the tagline sits below in a separate clean zone. The brand end-card is deliberately quieter and more symmetric than the feature montage.
- **Choreography:** 21.60–22.20: spark blooms and feature fragments fly toward the center in staggered timing. 22.20–23.15: two giant phrases replace one another with scale and tracking compression, not a slide change. 23.15–23.80: the X mark resolves and the full logo fades in, sharp and contrast-safe. 23.80–25.35: tagline unclips; a gentle network shimmer travels between X nodes while lockup remains readable. 25.35–26.00: central node shrinks to a point and title releases; on loop it matches the opening center node.
- **Transition contract:** End-to-start matches the logo's center node to shot 01's first visible network node, with no empty field.
- **Assets + technique:** Existing CampusX logo component duplicated locally without modifying product code, vector-led fragments and Space Grotesk. No external imagery.
- **Sound intent:** Resolved ending with a clean tail into the loop; music is the only audio layer.

## Pre-code variety review

Adjacent shots change field, hero scale, position and mechanic: mark construction → editorial feed → map route → diagonal market tiles → planner-to-chat transformation → centered brand lockup. Every shot has a post-entrance event, a readable resolved moment, and outgoing motion in its final 15–20%. Headline, callout and hero zones remain separate. No feature mockup claims to show live user data. Every shot has a visible beat in each second, with 0.4–0.5-second snaps at the opening. Graphic interface art is appropriate because the product is software, not a physical object.
